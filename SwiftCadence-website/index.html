Built for compliant messaging

SwiftCadence is designed to help businesses follow carrier rules and consumer consent requirements.

  • Consent requiredClients may only text contacts who have given the required permission to receive messages.
  • Automatic opt-outReplies like STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT are honored automatically, and the contact is never texted again by that business.
  • HELP supportReplying HELP returns business contact information.
  • Registered campaignsEvery business is registered with the carriers (10DLC) before sending.
  • No sale of mobile numbersWe never sell or share mobile numbers or opt-in data with third parties for their marketing. Read our Privacy Policy.
